TFT Archipelago

2026 · v0.5.5 · 88 downloads

A randomizer for Teamfight Tactics that plugs into Archipelago multiworlds. You start with no rerolls, no XP buys and two locked shop slots; items from other players' worlds unlock them. A Windows app reads the screen and the Riot client, covers locked buttons and checks each board at the end of a match. It never touches the game.

Better Speech Recognition

2026 · mod for Scam With Your Friends

The game turns your voice into text with a small Whisper model that mishears a lot, especially outside English. Press F8 in game to pick a bigger or language-specific model, download it, test your microphone and switch without a restart.

Blackout Mod

2025 · 333 downloads on Nexus

A mod for LOCKDOWN Protocol. The Dissident sabotages a defibrillator, the lights flicker, and the whole facility goes dark for every player in the lobby. The host sets the rules once, everyone else joins with a code. The sync runs over its own small server, not the game's netcode.

Blue Prince Wiki

2026 · 59 findings on our board

A shared notebook for a group playing the puzzle game Blue Prince. Take a photo of a note, a sheet of music or a map in the game, and Gemini reads the text off it and files it with a title, category and tags. Everyone on the board sees it, adds notes and checklists, and finds it again with search. A desktop version keeps everything on your own PC, without AI.

LectureAlly

2026 · 30 lecture languages

Upload lecture slides as a PDF and LectureAlly explains each slide out loud, with captions that follow every word, and answers questions as they come up. It runs on your own API key, has no accounts, and the lecture stays in your browser.

How I build

I make what I miss while playing: a rule the game lacks, a voice it mishears less, a power cut it never had.

Hands off the game
The TFT apps read the screen and the Riot client's own local API. They never read game memory or send input, so they stay clear of anti-cheat.
Open source
Everything listed here has its source on GitHub, so you can read what a mod does before you install it.
